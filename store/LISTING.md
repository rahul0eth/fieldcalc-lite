## Description (both stores)

FieldCalc is a fast, focused calculator for the jobsite. Pick a calculator, type your numbers, and read the answer — big and clear. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

TEN FIELD CALCULATORS

Building
• Area & Volume — length, width, and height in feet or inches → ft² and ft³
• Concrete — slab or footing volume in cubic yards, with waste and an 80-lb bag count
• Slope — rise over run → percent grade and angle
• Stairs — total rise and max riser → riser count and riser height

Mechanical
• Round Duct Airflow — diameter and velocity → CFM
• Rect → Round Duct — equivalent round diameter for a rectangular duct
• Pressure Conversion — inches water column → pascals and psi
• Pipe Volume — inside diameter and length → gallons and water weight

Electrical & units
• Voltage Drop — single or three phase, copper or aluminum, 14 AWG to 500 kcmil
• Feet-Inches → Metric — imperial lengths to millimeters and meters

FIELD LOG WITH PHOTOS
Save any result to your field log with one tap, or take a site photo with the camera and attach it to the calculation. Share any result to messages, email, or notes with the share sheet.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
FieldCalc has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your calculations and photos are stored only on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

IMPORTANT
FieldCalc is a convenience tool. Verify design and code calculations independently. It does not provide code compliance, engineering approval, or design suitability.
